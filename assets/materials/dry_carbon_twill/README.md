# Dry carbon twill appearance

Original 2×2 twill maps generated by [generate_carbon_weave.py](../../../tools/generate_carbon_weave.py), and a local MDL shader. All files in this directory are provided under Apache-2.0. No external texture download, asset-library installation or network access is needed at runtime.

The default 2 mm yarn pitch gives an 8 mm four-yarn repeat. The simulator derives normalized `st` UVs once from the rest sheet and scales the material by the rest dimensions, so the pattern moves with the cloth and keeps its physical scale across resolutions and sheet sizes. The MDL material blends two directional yarn highlights using a filtered warp/weft mask, with a modest normal relief and no resin coat. A `UsdPreviewSurface` fallback uses the same color, normal and roughness maps, with isotropic highlights.

Area-weighted vertex normals are refreshed with the rendered positions to avoid showing each physics triangle as a separate flat facet. This affects shading only, not cloth geometry or collision.

These are **illustrative, uncalibrated optical parameters**, not measured reflectance. The maps do not simulate individual filaments, actual thickness, porosity or fiber damage. Geometry remains two-sided; depth remains the simulator's geometric camera output. Newton's mass, stiffness, contact and damping settings are independent of this material and remain the existing uncalibrated baseline.

The PNGs are checked in. To regenerate them, with NumPy and Pillow installed:

```bash
python3 tools/generate_carbon_weave.py
```

`basecolor` is sRGB; `normal`, `roughness` and `warp_mask` are linear data. The normal map uses OpenGL (+Y) tangent-space convention. Preserve the MDL and its four PNG resources together when relocating the material.
